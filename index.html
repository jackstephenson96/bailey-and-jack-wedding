A few things about us

How we got here

First date

Our first date was on Lady Bird Lake in Jack's kayak. We listened to Latin music, shared a cigar, and apparently made a convincing enough case for a second date.

The proposal

For Bailey's birthday, her parents gave her a metal detector so she could look for treasure around the new house. Jack had already hidden one: a small chest buried in the yard, with the ring inside.

No toaster required

Our 1970s Home Fund

Before it became ours, our 1970s home had just one owner. We feel lucky to be part of its next chapter and want to care for it thoughtfully—making necessary repairs and updates while preserving the original character that made us love it. If you'd like to give a wedding gift, contributions to our home fund will help us continue showing this special house the love it deserves. Celebrating with us is more than enough.

Useful answers

Questions

What can we expect at the reception?

A relaxed backyard celebration with dinner, cocktails, dancing, live caricature artists, and a few surprises.

Will there be smoking?

An optional hookah lounge will be available in a designated outdoor area away from the dining space.

Can I bring my children?

Yes. Kids are welcome. Please include them in your RSVP.

Can I wear heels?

Yes, but the reception is outside on grass. Thick heels, wedges, boots, or flats will be much easier than stilettos.

Is the wedding outdoors?

Yes. The reception is a backyard celebration, so please check the forecast and bring a layer if the evening looks cool.

Can I park at the venue?

No guest parking is available on-site. Please use Uber or the hotel shuttle.

Can I take photos?

Yes. Take photos, have fun, and send us your favorites afterward.

Will vegetarian food be available?

Yes. Please also note any dietary restrictions when you RSVP.
